Ball Spotter

A friend films your shot, the app works out where it landed, then points you straight to it.

The first time, allow camera, microphone, location and motion when asked.

If you can read this line, the app's code isn't running. Open it from its https web address in Safari or Chrome, not from a file preview.

How it works

1. Line up. Your friend stands about 4 big steps straight behind the ball, looking down the target line, and holds the phone upright. They step forward or back until you fit inside the yellow box.

2. Ready. Tap READY. The app listens for the hit, then tries to trace the ball.

3. Mark. Your friend watches the ball land, puts the + on the spot where it came down and taps MARK. This is the most accurate step.

4. Distance. The app uses your club's usual carry. Adjust it with Short / Long or ±10 if the shot was mishit.

5. Find. Follow the big arrow. It beeps faster and talks as you get close. In the search zone, look in a circle about 15 yards wide.

Tip: If the arrow seems wrong, wave the phone in a figure 8 to calibrate the compass, and set magnetic declination in Settings.

Tap "Found it" when you find the ball. The app learns your real distance for that club.
